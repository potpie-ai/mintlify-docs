## What is Potpie

Potpie's proprietary [Context Engine](/concepts/context-engine) enables agents to understand your codebase in the depth required for complex **multi-hop** reasoning across components for **debugging**, **refactoring**, and other advanced tasks.
The **Spec driven development** workflow prioritizes upfront planning to define clear requirements and architecture so the code thats fits right into your codebase.
